### What we collect

- **You give us:** name, email address, phone number, and your answers to our
  application form or booking questions.
- **We generate:** the stage your application has reached, notes from our
  conversations, records of texts and calls between us, and call duration.
- **Automatically:** the marketing source that referred you (for example an
  Instagram campaign), and basic technical data when you use our site.

We do not collect special category data. Please don't send us health,
financial or other sensitive details — we have no need for them.

### Why we use it, and our legal basis

| What we do | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Reply to your application and arrange a call | Taking steps at your request before entering a contract |
| Send you SMS updates and follow-ups about the programme | **Your consent** |
| Call you about your application | Our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries |
| Record calls, where enabled | **Your consent**, given at the start of the call |
| Deliver the programme if you join | Performance of our contract with you |
| Keep invoices and financial records | Legal obligation |
| Keep security and access logs | Our legitimate interest in protecting your data |

### Withdrawing consent

**Reply STOP to any text** and we stop immediately — it's automatic. You can
also email us at any time. Withdrawing consent doesn't affect anything done
beforehand, and it won't stop us replying to something you asked us about.

### How long we keep it

- **Applicants who don't join:** **60 days** after our last contact
- **Members:** for the duration of the programme, then **[24]** months
- **Financial records:** 6 years, as tax law requires
- **Call recordings, if enabled:** **[90]** days
- **Suppression records:** kept indefinitely — this is how we make sure a
  deletion request is honoured permanently and you aren't added back

### Your rights

You can ask us to: give you a copy of your data, correct it, delete it,
restrict or object to how we use it, or send it to another provider.

We respond within **one month**. There's no charge.

We can provide a complete export of everything we hold about you, and deletion
removes your record and message history from our systems entirely.
